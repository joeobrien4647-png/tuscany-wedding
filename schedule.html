Good to Know

Weather

Late May in Tuscany is usually warm and sunny (20 to 25°C). Sunglasses and sunscreen are a good idea, and bring a light layer for the evening.

Children

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you the chance to let your hair down!
