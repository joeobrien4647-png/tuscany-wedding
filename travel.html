Getting There

Your guide to travelling to Tuscany

Nearest Airports

Five airports are within reach of La Conca. Drive times are rough, and which one suits you best depends on where you're flying from.

Perugia (PEG)

Closest

A small, easy airport just over the border in Umbria. It has fewer flights than the others, so check it first.

Drive to La Conca: about 55 minutes

Florence (FLR)

Small and quick to get through.

Drive to La Conca: about 1 hour 45 minutes

Bologna (BLQ)

A bigger airport with plenty of routes.

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hours

Pisa (PSA)

Lots of budget flights, but further west, so a longer drive.

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hours 15 minutes

Rome (FCO or CIA)

Fiumicino is Italy's biggest airport, and Ciampino is the smaller one. The longest drive, but handy if you want a few days in Rome too.

Drive to La Conca: about 3 hours

Getting Around

Once you've landed, here's how to get to La Conca.

Car Hire

The simplest way to get from the airport to La Conca, and handy if you want to explore. The big rental companies are at all the airports.

Tip: Share a car with friends on the same flight and split the cost. Automatics are available but usually cost more, so book early if you want one.

Private Transfer

Sit back and enjoy the scenery. Several companies run private transfers from the airports.

Tip: Book in advance, and share one with others landing around the same time.

Train + Taxi

Possible, but fiddly. The nearest main-line station is Arezzo, about 40 minutes' drive from La Conca, so you'd need a taxi from there.

Tip: Trains are run by Trenitalia and Italo. Sort the taxi out before you travel.

Travel Tips

When should I book flights? +

Late May is a popular time in Tuscany, and the Friday before the Spring Bank Holiday is a busy day to fly from the UK, so book as early as you can.

Do I need an international driving licence? +

If you have a UK photocard licence, you can drive in Italy with it. If your licence is from somewhere else, check whether you also need an International Driving Permit.

Is there parking at the venue? +

Yes. Both villas have their own private parking, so you can park right where you're staying.

Can I make a longer trip of it? +

Please do. Your bed at La Conca is yours from Friday to Monday, and there's plenty of Tuscany and Umbria to see either side of the weekend. Just let us know your plans so we know when to expect you.

Weather in May

Late May in Tuscany is usually warm and sunny, around 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) by day. Pack sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light layer for evenings.

Currency & Payments

Italy uses the Euro (EUR). Credit cards are widely accepted, but smaller shops and trattorias may prefer cash.

Tips:
- Use a fee-free card like Monzo or Starling
- ATMs (Bancomat) are common in towns
- Tipping isn't expected, but appreciated
