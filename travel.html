Getting There

Your guide to travelling to Tuscany

Where to Fly To

Eight airports are within reach of La Conca, listed here by how long the drive takes without traffic. Which one suits you depends on where you're flying from.

Perugia (PEG)

Closest

A small, easy airport just over the border in Umbria, with only a few UK flights.

  • Ryanair from Stansted
  • British Airways from Heathrow, but it flies Thu 27 May and not on Fri 28 or Mon 31

Drive to La Conca: about 1 hr 10 min

Florence (FLR)

Small and quick to get through.

  • British Airways from Heathrow and London City
  • Vueling from Gatwick

Drive to La Conca: about 1 hr 45 min

Rimini (RMI)

On the Adriatic coast, over the hills to the east.

  • British Airways from Heathrow (Thu 27 out, Mon 31 back)
  • easyJet from Gatwick, Wednesdays and Saturdays only
  • Ryanair from Stansted: summer 2027 not on sale yet, book when fares load

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hrs

Ancona (AOI)

Small, on the Adriatic coast.

  • Ryanair from Stansted

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hrs 20 min

Bologna (BLQ)

A bigger airport with plenty of routes.

  • British Airways from Heathrow
  • Ryanair from Stansted
  • Ryanair from Luton, Manchester and Edinburgh: summer 2027 not on sale yet

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hrs 20 min

Pisa (PSA)

The most UK routes, but further west, so a longer drive.

  • easyJet from Gatwick, Luton, Southend, Bristol and Manchester
  • Ryanair from Stansted
  • British Airways from Heathrow
  • Jet2 also sells summer 2027 flights to Pisa: check jet2.com
  • Ryanair from Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham and Prestwick: summer 2027 not on sale yet

Drive to La Conca: about 2 hrs 50 min

Rome (CIA or FCO)

The longest drive, but handy if you want a few days in Rome too.

  • Ciampino: Ryanair from Stansted, Manchester and Edinburgh
  • Fiumicino: British Airways and ITA from Heathrow; easyJet from Gatwick, Bristol, Birmingham, Manchester, Glasgow, Newcastle and Belfast; Wizz Air from Luton
  • From Boston: Delta and ITA fly direct to Fiumicino

Drive to La Conca: Ciampino about 3 hrs 5 min, Fiumicino about 3 hrs 40 min

Before You Book

Five things to check first. The passport one catches people out.

Your passport
It has to pass two checks. It must be less than 10 years old on the day you land: issued on or after 29 May 2017 if you land on Fri 28 May, or on or after 28 May 2017 if you land on Thu 27 May. It must also be valid until at least 1 September 2027. This applies to UK and US passports. If yours fails either check, renew it this autumn, before the spring rush. UK government guidance
ETIAS
Not live yet, so there's nothing to do. We'll tell you if that changes before May. Any website charging for ETIAS now is a scam.
New passport checks (EES)
The first time you enter, border staff take your fingerprints and a photo. It's free and there's nothing to do in advance, but queues can be long, so allow extra time when you land and when you fly home.
Health cover and travel insurance
From the UK: get a free GHIC from the NHS website (it's free, so ignore sites that charge), and buy travel insurance when you book your flights, declaring any medical conditions. From the US: your health plan may not cover you in Italy, so get travel medical cover that includes getting you home.
Hiring a car
Park outside the town walls. Sansepolcro and most Italian towns have camera-enforced ZTL zones, and driving past a ZTL sign means a fine that can arrive months later, so don't follow the satnav through one. A UK photocard licence is fine; US drivers should carry an International Driving Permit (from AAA) with their licence.

Getting Around

Once you've landed, here's how to get to La Conca.

Car Hire

The simplest way to get from the airport to La Conca, and handy if you want to explore. The big rental companies are at all the airports.

Tip: Share a car with friends on the same flight and split the cost. Automatics are available but usually cost more, so book early if you want one.

Private Transfer

Sit back and enjoy the scenery. Several companies run private transfers from the airports.

Tip: Book in advance, and share one with others landing around the same time.

Train + Taxi

Possible, but fiddly. The nearest main-line station is Arezzo, about 40 minutes' drive from La Conca, so you'd need a taxi from there.

Tip: Trains are run by Trenitalia and Italo. Sort the taxi out before you travel.

Travel Tips

When should I book flights? +

As soon as your route is on sale. Most are already, and Friday 28 May is the start of a bank holiday weekend, so fares on the busiest days tend to rise as seats sell. A few Ryanair routes open later in the winter: the search above shows which.

Do I need an international driving licence? +

If you have a UK photocard licence, you can drive in Italy with it. If your licence is from somewhere else, check whether you also need an International Driving Permit.

Is there parking at the venue? +

Yes. Both villas have their own private parking, so you can park right where you're staying.

Can I make a longer trip of it? +

Please do. Your bed at La Conca is yours from Friday to Monday, and there's plenty of Tuscany and Umbria to see either side of the weekend. Just let us know your plans so we know when to expect you.

Practical Information

Everything you need for a smooth trip to Tuscany.

Weather in May

Late May in Tuscany is usually warm and sunny, around 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) by day. Pack sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light layer for evenings.

Currency & Payments

Italy uses the Euro (EUR). Credit cards are widely accepted, but smaller shops and trattorias may prefer cash.

Tips:
- Use a fee-free card like Monzo or Starling
- ATMs (Bancomat) are common in towns
- Tipping isn't expected, but appreciated
