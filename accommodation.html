Where You'll Stay

Everyone together, on site, at La Conca

Stay at La Conca

We've taken over two old stone villas at Agriturismo La Conca for the whole weekend, so that everyone can stay together, on site, from Friday to Monday. No hotels, no taxis home, everyone in one place.

We'll sort out who sleeps where, so there's nothing for you to book, and we'll let you know which villa you're in nearer the time.

Villa Santarsa

Sleeps 31
Private pool Pizza oven & barbecue Views over the lake

A stone hamlet dating back to the 1500s, at the top of the estate, with views across Lago di Montedoglio and the valley. Thirteen bedrooms, its own pool and a wood-fired pizza oven.

More info: View on the La Conca website

Villa Vallorsaia

Sleeps 26
Private pool Pizza oven & barbecue Valley views

The oldest farmhouse on the estate: a stone house tucked into the greenery, with its own pool, a wood-fired oven and a big covered area for eating outside. Ten bedrooms.

More info: View on the La Conca website

What It Costs

£150 per person

For the whole weekend. That covers your bed for the three nights. Once you've registered and said you're coming, the details for the £150 appear on your page when you sign in. No rush.

Everything else is on us

From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave: every meal from Friday night to Monday morning, every drink, the pools, the grounds, the lot.

No presents

Please, genuinely, no presents. You being there is the present.

Questions?

If stairs or anything else would make a room tricky, tell us on the register page (there's a box for it) and we'll bear it in mind. For anything else, message Sophie or Joe, or have a look at the FAQs.
