Frequently Asked Questions

How do I RSVP? +

The register page is the RSVP. Register, tell us whether you're coming, and let us know about anything you can't eat or anything that would make the weekend easier. Please do it as soon as you know.

If your plans change, sign in and update your answer there.

Where will we stay? +

With us. Everyone stays together on site, in two old stone villas at La Conca: Villa Santarsa and Villa Vallorsaia.

We'll sort out the rooms and let you know which villa you're in nearer the time, so there's nothing for you to book.

What does it cost? +

£150 per person for the whole weekend. That covers your bed for the three nights. Everything else, from the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, is on us: every meal from Friday night to Monday morning, every drink, the pools, the lot.

Once you've registered and said you're coming, the details for the £150 appear on your page when you sign in. No rush.

What about gifts? +

Please, genuinely, no presents. You being there is the present.

When should we arrive and leave? +

Arrive on Friday 28 May and leave on Monday 31 May. Your bed is yours for all three nights, and the wedding is on Saturday 29 May.

Can I bring my children? +

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you the chance to have a proper holiday and let your hair down!

What's the weather like in May? +

Late May in Tuscany is usually warm and sunny, around 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) by day.

We recommend bringing sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light layer for the evening when it can get a bit cooler.

Do I need to speak Italian? +

Not at all! English is widely spoken in tourist areas of Tuscany. That said, learning a few basic phrases like "Grazie" (thank you) and "Buongiorno" (good morning) is always appreciated by locals.

Can you cater for my diet? +

Yes. Tell us what you can't eat when you register (there's a box for it on the register page) and we'll make sure you're looked after.

Is there parking at the venue? +

Yes. Both villas have their own private parking, so if you're driving, you can park right where you're staying.

Is there Wi-Fi? +

Yes, there's free Wi-Fi in both villas.

I have more questions! +

No problem. Message Sophie or Joe as you normally would, or have a look at the contact page.

Accessibility Information

We want everyone to be comfortable. Here's what we know so far, and how to tell us what you need.

♿

Getting Around

La Conca is a country estate on a hillside, so expect some slopes and uneven ground. If steps or distances would be tricky, tell us and we'll bear it in mind when we sort the rooms.

🚗

Parking

Both villas have their own private parking. If you need to park close to your door, just let us know.

🍽

Dietary Needs

Allergies, intolerances or preferences: tell us on the register page (there's a box for it) and we'll make sure you're looked after.

🔊

Hearing & Visual

If you have hearing or visual needs, let us know so we can make sure you can see and hear everything during the ceremony.

🩹

Medical Needs

Please bring any medication you need. The nearest hospital with an A&E is Ospedale della Valtiberina in Sansepolcro. In an emergency, call 112.

The easiest way to tell us is the box on the register page, or just message Sophie or Joe.
